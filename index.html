About

I spent a decade writing production software. Now I build the AI and data systems that keep businesses running on it.

I'm Harrison Smith, an independent AI and data engineer based in Ogden, Utah, operating under HSmithDev LLC. My background is classic software engineering: backend services, SQL systems, CI/CD pipelines. The last several years, I've rebuilt that foundation around AI, with RAG pipelines, multi-model orchestration across Claude, Gemini, and ChatGPT, local LLM hosting, and the data engineering that makes any of it trustworthy.

Day to day, I'm the Business Intelligence Analyst at BlytzPay, a Series A payments company, where I build the BigQuery models and dashboards Finance and Risk run on, and own the evaluation framework for their production AI agent. I bring that same production discipline to client work.

My M.S. in Applied AI pushed me deep into the parts a bootcamp skips: model training and evaluation, ETL design, and the statistics behind knowing whether a model actually works. I build with Claude Code as a first-class part of my workflow, to move faster through engineering, not skip it.
